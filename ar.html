Clarity – AR Migraine Prevention & Relief

Challenge

Migraine detection today relies on apps, smartwatches, and biometric data that track sleep, heart rate, activity, and stress. While useful, these tools miss two of the most common and critical triggers:

  • Bright light exposure — affects 80–90% of migraine sufferers
  • Posture and neck strain — affects up to 60% of patients

These triggers are difficult to notice personally and nearly impossible for phones or wearables to capture. Sudden brightness changes, flicker, glare, and poor head position often influence the onset long before symptoms appear.

“Migraine is one of the leading causes of years lived with disability worldwide.”

The Lancet Neurology, 2022

Global Burden of Disease Study: Migraine Analysis

Solution

Clarity is an AR-powered system that predicts migraine risk and provides real-time interventions. Using Snap Spectacles, it combines machine learning on wearable and environmental data with interactive interventions to reduce pain.

  • Stage 1: Predicts migraine onset using personal patterns from light exposure, posture, activity, stress, and environmental data.
  • Stage 2: Offers interventions through AR: dimming harmful light, guided meditation with visual capsules, or the 5-4-3-2-1 method for pain reduction.

The system runs silently in the background and notifies the user only when high-risk conditions are detected, suggesting personalized mitigation strategies.

Impact and reflection

Within 48 hours, the prototype achieved Top 10 in the Snapchat challenge at Junction Hackathon. Clarity demonstrated the potential for AR to detect hidden migraine triggers and deliver personalized interventions.
